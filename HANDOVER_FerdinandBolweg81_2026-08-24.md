## 11. Lessons worth keeping

- Independent verification beat every summary: the "€19k MATIG" narrative that scared off the first buyer became €2.9k under a proper NTA 8060 inspection with correct communal attribution.
- The VvE was the decisive quality signal (professional administration, €112k reserve, funded works) — the same street two doors down (nr 35) failed on a dormant VvE.
- Contract hygiene mattered: three drafts contained a words/figures price mismatch, a wrong deed number, a receipt declaration for a non-existent document, no spousal consent and no Vormerkung — all fixed before signature.
- Foundation certainty was cheap (Fase 0 attest) and removed the last structural unknown.
