## 5. Bidding advice

- The €545k ask is bait pricing; expect multiple bidders. Plan for **inschrijving**; ask Ludenhoff for procedure/deadline immediately.
- **Recommended bid: ≈ €575,000** (+5.5%) — sits mid fair-value band, competitive vs. comps at €5,476/m² (still below buurt average for turnkey homes).
- If competition is confirmed heavy (agent signals many bids): stretch to **€585–590k**. **Walk-away: €600k** — above that, €600k + €60–80k renovation ≥ buying already-renovated (cf. Noorderkroon 40 economics).
- **Conditions:** keep financing voorbehoud as needed; do the bouwkundige keuring *before* the deadline (~€450) so the bid can be clean on inspection — materially stronger, and prudent given the ouderdomsclausule risk. Offer a **flexible transfer date** — likely valuable to the family of the elderly seller and costs nothing.
- Before bidding, ask the makelaar: (1) bidding procedure & deadline; (2) concept-koopakte clauses (ouderdoms-/niet-zelfbewonings-/asbest); (3) parking-spot kettingbeding status; (4) bathroom/electrics age; (5) why viewing not yet planned in DB — schedule one ASAP.
