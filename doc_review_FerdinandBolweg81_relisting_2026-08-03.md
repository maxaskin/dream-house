## 2. New findings from this re-read

1. **Rookgaskanalen / CV-replacement bottleneck (new, time-bound).** ALV 09-04-2026: the chimney renovation explicitly **excludes the rookgaskanalen**, and per current rules a CV ketel may not be replaced (where the work touches the collective flue) without a valid inspection report. The VvE only decided "to investigate further". FB81's ketel is from **2012** — realistically due for replacement within ~3–6 years. If the flues fail inspection, the VvE must repair first (extra capex, delay). *Action: ask the VvE/beheerder for the flue-inspection plan and timing.*
2. **Energy label fine print.** Heat demand 154.65 kWh/m² — **fails the Standaard voor woningisolatie**; renewables 0.0%; **summer overheating risk rated HOOG** (both bedrooms under the zadeldak). House rules permit airco only at the rear / on the dormer, boxed (anthracite), ≤48 dB(A), with gebruikersovereenkomst + kettingbeding. Budget for this if summer comfort matters.
3. **Three different floor areas in the file.** Energy label **95 m²** · Funda/brochure **89 m²** · BAG/kadastraal **86 m²**. At ~€5.4k/m² the 86-vs-89 gap alone is ~€16k of paper value. The **NEN2580 meetrapport is non-negotiable** before/with the bid.
4. **Standleidingen remain unquantified.** Cast iron, ±30-year cycle exceeded, none replaced anywhere in the VvE yet; one portiek already has a defective horizontal line (urgent). No offers yet; an **extra ALV** will decide (replacement vs relining, Tubus consulted). A buyer inherits an open-ended decision. *Ask: which portiek is defective, and the expected per-unit cost.* Keep the €5–15k contingency.
5. **Insurance gap now verified, not just suspected.** The 2022 Meijers pakketpolis (all-risks, insured sum €6,891,300, index 114.5, taxatie Slager 07-09-2021) contains **no appartementenclausule** — the seller confirms this (Q15b "nee"). Also the valuation is from 2021; construction costs have risen substantially since. *Actions: 2026 polisblad, confirm automatic indexation, push (via VvE) to add the appartementenclausule; confirm foundation cover.*
6. **Roof disclosures (minor but honest).** Old dry stain at the chimney channel in the rear bedroom (presumed historic mini-leak; the VvE-wide chimney renovation addresses the cause); a historic leak where the neighbours' dormer trim wasn't sealed (dry for years); screw tips protrude through the roof deck in the vliering (2015 works, cosmetic/safety nuisance). Rain caps were **not** included in the chimney quotes — a cheap prevention measure the VvE skipped.
7. **Governance is thin.** Board down to **one person**; the technical commission was reinforced by the owners of **81 (the seller — departs on sale)** and 73; an external paid board was explicitly mentioned as fallback (would raise costs). Recurring dissatisfaction with VvE Beheer Groep (2025: considered switching; 2026: staff churn, slow repair follow-ups). Watch item, not a red flag.
8. **Fire safety/BBL:** members chose **not** to commission a BBL/fire-safety report (no legal obligation yet) — defensible, but insurers increasingly ask for it.
9. **Positives re-confirmed:** very low actual energy use (gas 411 m³, electricity 1,126 kWh, ±€80/mo for both — the C label lives well); lijst van zaken is generous (kitchen appliances, curtains, floors, design radiator, screens all stay; nothing leased); electrical partly renewed (extra group 2015, smart meter); running costs modest (OZB €289, parking permit €40/yr, 1 per address).
10. **Formality:** the Vragenlijst copy in the pack is **unsigned** — get the signed version attached to the koopakte.

## 4. Price view for the relisting

Value anchors are unchanged since the 08-07 second check: WOZ €471k (peildatum 01-01-2025, next beschikking trending ~€500k) · Walter Living ~€500–511k point estimate (€477–610k range) · FB73 (same VvE, renovated) sold Jan-2026 off a €550k ask ≈ €6.3k/m² · Stadshart still overbids on average (≈68% above ask, ~+€17k) · **but**: 53 days on market, one failed transaction, and FB35 (two doors down, label D, weaker VvE) is back as the alternative at €485k.

**Fair value with a clean survey: ~€485,000–500,000.** The €479k ask remains keen against the anchors, but the relisting kills any premium: relisted-after-fall-through properties rarely spark bidding wars, and every week on the market strengthens the buyer.

**Bid strategy (BID — conditional, unchanged in direction, tightened in level):**

- **Open €465,000–470,000** — arguments: failed sale + 53 days, BAG 86 m², unquantified standleidingen decision pending, rookgaskanaal/CV bottleneck, no appartementenclausule, cosmetics budget.
- **Target €475,000–482,000.** Only go to/above ask if a second bidder verifiably materialises.
- **Ceiling €490,000** (was €495k in July — a property burned once does not warrant a premium). Above €490k the VvE capex pipeline eats the upside; above €505k it's overpaying on every anchor.
- **Walk away** if: the prior fall-through traces to a survey finding the seller won't document; eigen grond fails at the akte; the keuring finds foundation/structural movement; or a near-term standleidingen levy >€15k for nr 81 is confirmed.

**Conditions in the koopakte (non-negotiable):**
1. Bouwkundige keuring (incl. crawl space/foundation, asbestos scan).
2. Kadaster + leveringsakte: volle eigendom, achterpad-erfdienstbaarheid, berging named per splitsingstekening.
3. NEN2580 meetrapport (86/89/95 discrepancy).
4. VvE pack: jaarrekening 2025 (approved 04-2026), current reserve stand, zero-arrears verklaring for A-21, standleidingen status/offers + which portiek is defective, rookgaskanaal-inspection plan.
5. 2026 polisblad with indexed insured sum; appartementenclausule (or VvE commitment to add).
6. Signed Vragenlijst B + written confirmation the previous koopovereenkomst is dissolved + written answer whether the previous buyer performed a keuring and what it found (mededelingsplicht).
7. Financing clause per mortgage situation.

**Bottom line:** the document pack survives a second, harder read: honest seller, genuinely strong VvE fundamentals for this price class, freehold, sharp €/m². The risk picture has shifted from "verify tenure" (June) to "VvE forward capex + why did the deal die". Bid, but let the fall-through answer set the tone: financing failure → proceed to target zone; survey finding → reprice or walk.
